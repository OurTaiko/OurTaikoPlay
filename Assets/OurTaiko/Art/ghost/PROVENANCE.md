# ゴーストバトル art

Every image in this folder was drawn for OurTaikoPlay by `Documentation/ghost-art.swift`
(CoreGraphics shapes only; it reads no other artwork): the list's night background, the mode's
mark, the ghost face shown for a recorded player, the wisp, the VS emblem and Entry's mode board.
They are part of the game and under its license (GPL-3.0). The plates, panels and icons the screen
shares with マルチ演奏 are in `Assets/OurTaiko/Art/multi`.

To draw them again: `swift Documentation/ghost-art.swift Assets/OurTaiko/Art/ghost` (macOS).
