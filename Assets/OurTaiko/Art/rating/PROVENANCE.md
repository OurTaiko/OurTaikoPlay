# Chart rating art

Every image in this folder was drawn for OurTaikoPlay by `Documentation/rating-art.swift`
(CoreGraphics shapes only; it reads no other artwork): the three faces of the result screen's
"how was this chart?" prompt (liked it, not now, did not like it). They are part of the game and
under its license (GPL-3.0). The prompt's plates, panel and focus ring are マルチ演奏's
(`Assets/OurTaiko/Art/multi`).

To draw them again: `swift Documentation/rating-art.swift Assets/OurTaiko/Art/rating` (macOS).
The import settings are in each image's `.meta`.
