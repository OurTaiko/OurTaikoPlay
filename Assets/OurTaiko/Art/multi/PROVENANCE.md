# マルチ演奏 art

Every image in this folder was drawn for OurTaikoPlay by `Documentation/multi-art.swift`
(CoreGraphics shapes only; it reads no other artwork): the room background, the panels and plates,
the icons, Entry's mode board and the sixteen stamps. They are part of the game and under its
license (GPL-3.0).

To draw them again: `swift Documentation/multi-art.swift Assets/OurTaiko/Art/multi` (macOS).
The sprite borders and import settings are in each image's `.meta`.
